JSW OGPR Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

JSW OGPR is an application used for managing and viewing OGPR records. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the JSW OGPR application.

Information We Collect

The application may store information entered by authorized users into the OGPR records, including names, record numbers, dates, remarks and other information entered into the application.

Firebase

JSW OGPR uses Firebase services to store and synchronize application data. Information entered into the application may be transmitted to Firebase for storage and synchronization.

Notifications

The application may use notifications to inform users about relevant changes or activities within the application.

PDF Downloads

The application may allow users to generate and download PDF files containing information displayed in the application.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services required for data storage, synchronization and application functionality.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the JSW OGPR application, please contact the application administrator.
